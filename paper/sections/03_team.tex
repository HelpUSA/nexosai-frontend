\section{Team}

The team brings practical experience in systems, infrastructure, automation, databases, digital operations, and in the development of the NexusAI prototype. This combination enables a deep understanding of the problem faced by consultancies, IT teams, and small businesses that wish to use AI in real operations with security, traceability, and control.

The project already has a functional technical base, which reduces execution risk and increases the feasibility of evolving into an MVP. In addition to technical expertise, the proposal includes validation with local pilots, iterations with users, and support from partners to complement market, user experience, and commercial positioning aspects. This makes the team fit to develop, test, validate, and transform the solution into a product.

Wagner Medeiros dos Santos, founder of HelpUS LLC (Gulf Shores, AL), is in charge of conception and development of the NexusAI prototype, focusing on integrating AI, controlled execution, traceability, operational memory, and auditing.
