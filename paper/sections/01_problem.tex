\section{Oportunity/Challenge}

Consultancies, IT teams, and small businesses already use generative AI to research, draft, and analyze information. However, they still face difficulty transforming these responses into real, secure, and auditable operations.

Rototines such as technical documentation, file verification, database queries, assisted script execution, and record of evidence still depend on copying and pasting between systems, dispersed manual validations, and incomplete histories. This generates rework, delays, operational errors, and loss of knowledge.

Consequently, there is a market opportunity for a platform that enables AI usage with operational memory, permissions, traceability, and human validation, reducing execution time and increasing productivity, reliability, and governance in digital operations.
