\section{Market and Scalability}

The platform is designed to serve consultancies, IT teams, and small businesses with recurring digital processes - including sectors such as technology, health, legal, and education.

The demand is driven by the rapid and growing use of generative AI, the pressure to automate while preserving auditability, and the need to reduce the time and effort lost on manual copy-and-paste between systems. 

The core technology is independent of selected markets, enabling practical expansion to new segments without structural rework. The business model contemplates subscriptions for companies and operational teams, individual user licenses, and enterprise Slas agreements.

The base technology remains the same.
